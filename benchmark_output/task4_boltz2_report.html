Key Insights

Strong Energetic Correlation: A robust negative correlation (r = -0.689, p = 1.98e-140) exists between PyRosetta interface ΔG and the number of hydrogen bonds. This indicates that hydrogen bonding contributes significantly to interface stability, with each additional H-bond contributing approximately -4.54 kcal/mol of favorable binding energy.
Model Discrimination Power: Boltz2 confidence scores are significantly different between binder and non-binder samples (independent t-test, p < 3.68e-07). The mean confidence for binders (0.862 ± 0.081) is notably higher than for non-binders (0.836 ± 0.076), suggesting the model can discriminate binding interfaces from non-binding ones.
Target Coverage: The dataset spans 20 targets (egfr, nipah-glycoprotein-g, il7r being the most represented). Each target received multiple samples, providing sufficient statistical power for per-target analysis.
  • Mean Boltz2 confidence score: 0.8486 (SD = 0.0792), indicating generally reliable predictions across the dataset.
  • Interface hydrogen bonds range from 0 to 28, with a median of 8 per interface.
  • PyRosetta interface ΔG ranges from -243.26 to -2.53 kcal/mol, with binders tending toward more negative (favorable) values.
  • Shape complementarity (boltz2_binder_ss) ranges from 0.32 to 0.78, with a mean of 0.598.
